Caster needed to feel immediately recognizable while still carrying the mystery of a character whose identity is partly constructed. The final design uses the tall hat, glasses, and layered silhouette as strong visual anchors, while the expression sheet keeps that guarded personality readable in dialogue.

Proto’s design problem was balancing an approachable, unfinished quality with the unease of something made for a purpose. Simplified shapes and an open silhouette keep the character readable, while the process studies explore how posture and costume can suggest vulnerability.

Collector needed to read as imposing without becoming visually flat or anonymous. The final design uses a strong coat-and-mask silhouette, while the process and expression studies test how small changes in eyes, pose, and proportion can reveal the character beneath the uniform.
