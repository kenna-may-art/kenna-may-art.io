PROJECT OVERVIEW & RESPONSIBILITIES

My goal was to build a visually cohesive game demo with a mysterious industrial theme with bio-mechanical components. Because I was working independently, I developed a reusable production pipeline for character sprites, environments, interface assets, and narrative implementation.

RESPONSIBILITIES

As a solo developer, I directed the visual language and handled character design, environment art, UI design, narrative design, implementation, and promotional assets.

CHARACTER DESIGN: CASTER

Caster's silhouette balances theatrical authority with subtle constraint. His attire and mannerisms communicate control, while the damaged mechanical parts hint that beneath it all, the performance is unstable. Or falling apart.

View more

ENVIRONMENT DESIGN: THE CLOCK SHOP

The shop needed to feel cluttered worn without interfering with readability. I reserved low contrast areas behind the character sprites and concentrated important props around exploration points.

UI & UX DESIGN

A simple yet unique UI keeps dialogue and player controls clear over dark backgrounds. Keeping the main focus on the game itself.

WHAT I LEARNED

Making Mechanical Heart taught me how to maintain visual consistency across characters, environments, and UI while managing the scope of an independent production. It challenged me to balance my creative side with the practicalities of a solo development process.

I learned to prototype, revise, and adapt. All to deliver an enjoyable experience.
