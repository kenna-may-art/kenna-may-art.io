01 / Character art

A ringmaster with something to hide.

The tall hat, crimson coat, and pale half mask give Caster a recognizable silhouette across the game’s dark backgrounds.

Caster / The ringmaster

Composed at a glance.
Uneasy up close.

I wanted Caster to feel theatrical without losing the subtle expressions needed for a dialogue scene. The costume carries his stage presence; the face carries the tension.

01

Readable shapeThe hat and layered coat anchor the silhouette.

02

Controlled paletteDeep reds connect him to the circus and interface.

03

Dialogue presenceSmall shifts in his expression sit against the formal costume.
