01 / Finished work

Art built for the story

Characters, spaces, and interface elements share a theatrical, worn industrial palette. Each piece has to hold up on its own and remain readable in a dialogue scene.

01 / Character design

Caster

A theatrical silhouette and restrained expression make the ringmaster feel composed at a glance. The half mask, dark layers, and red coat carry the tension underneath that performance.

02 / Environment art

Ash & Cog Repairs

The clock shop gives the story a lived-in starting place. I shaped the lighting and clusters of props around the room’s focal points, leaving space for characters and dialogue.

03 / Interface design

UI & UX Design

I explored menu hierarchy, recurring card motifs, and transition imagery before settling on the title screen used in the demo. The interface carries the same theatrical, mechanical tone as the characters and environments.

03 / About the artist

Hi, I’m Kenna.

I’m a 2D game artist and multimedia designer based in Anaheim, California. I like story-driven games where characters, environments, interface, and sound all help create the same feeling.

Mechanical Heart is my solo project. I’ve handled its visual development, narrative implementation, UI, promotional art, and audio. I’m looking for junior game art and multimedia roles where I can contribute across disciplines and keep growing as a visual developer.
