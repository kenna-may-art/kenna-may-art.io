About me

Hi, I’m Kenna.

I’m a 2D game artist and multimedia designer based in Anaheim, California. I’m interested in story-driven games where characters, environments, interface, and sound all help create the same feeling.

I made Mechanical Heart as a solo developer, from visual development through narrative implementation and audio. I’m seeking junior game art and multimedia roles where I can contribute across disciplines and keep growing as a visual developer.
